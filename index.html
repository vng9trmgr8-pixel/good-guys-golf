Our Mission

Three Decades of Keeping Kids on the Field

Good Guys Golf is the longest-running charity golf tournament in Chico, California — and for 30 years, every tee shot and every putt has been for the same reason: the kids. What started as a small group of friends in 1995 has grown into a beloved annual tradition at Butte Creek Country Club.

Every dollar raised goes directly to local Little League and youth sports — Westside, Central, and Eastside Little League, Chico Softball Little League, Jr. Golf, Cast Hope, and Chico United Football Club. Because when kids have a team, they have a future.
